01 · ABOUT ME

个人简介About Me

中国农业大学土地科学与技术学院博士研究生，主要从事无人机遥感、土地信息技术与农业水资源高效利用研究。研究聚焦无人机热红外和多光谱遥感在作物水分胁迫诊断、生长状态监测及精准灌溉中的应用，参与国家小麦产业技术体系、中国农业科学院青年创新专项及国家自然科学基金项目，在 Agricultural Water Management、Ecological Engineering 和 Plants 等期刊发表相关研究成果。

Zhenhao Gao is a Ph.D. student at the College of Land Science and Technology, China Agricultural University. His research interests include UAV remote sensing, land information technology, and efficient use of agricultural water resources, with an emphasis on thermal and multispectral remote sensing for crop water-stress diagnosis, crop monitoring, and precision irrigation.
